THE WORKFORCE IMPERATIVE

Technical capability is only part of workforce readiness.

AI is changing how work is performed, while human capability continues to shape how people communicate, collaborate, lead, think and respond to changing conditions.

Corporate Engagement translates ISSR 2030 research into a structured conversation about the human capabilities that matter within your workforce, leadership pipeline and future capability strategy.

CORPORATE CAPABILITY FRAMEWORK

From workforce readiness to human capability.

01

Workforce Readiness

Examine the human capabilities employees need alongside technical and functional capability as work continues to evolve.

02

Communication Capability

Understand communication across professional, managerial, meeting, presentation and high-pressure contexts.

03

Leadership Capability

Explore the communication, thinking and people capabilities required across emerging and experienced leadership roles.

04

Critical Thinking

Consider how employees process information, respond to complexity and think while communicating and making decisions.

05

Adaptability

Identify capability areas associated with changing roles, technologies, expectations and workplace environments.

06

Capability Development

Translate identified readiness priorities into structured development pathways, practice, evidence and reassessment.
