THE EXECUTIVE BRIEFING

From research findings to leadership understanding.

ISSR 2030 brings together practitioner observation, field evidence and global research to examine the human capabilities shaping people, work and organisations in an AI-accelerated world.

The Executive Briefing provides a focused interpretation of these findings for leaders who need to understand what the changing human-skill landscape may mean for their institution, workforce or talent strategy.

BRIEFING FRAMEWORK

What the briefing explores.

01

The Human-Skill Shift

How the changing workplace is increasing the importance of human capability alongside technical capability.

02

ISSR 2030 Findings

The principal research signals and capability patterns emerging from the ISSR 2030 framework.

03

Stakeholder Implications

What the findings mean for employers, institutions, employees and students.

04

From Insight to Action
