Build Human-Skill Readiness Across the Education Journey.

Explore Education Engagement → Explore ISSR 2030

THE EDUCATION IMPERATIVE

Readiness begins before the workplace.

ISSR 2030 examines the human capabilities that increasingly shape readiness in an AI-accelerated world.

Education is where many of these capabilities are developed, practiced and strengthened. Education Engagement translates the research into practical conversations for school leaders, higher education leaders, academic teams, employability leaders and other decision-makers responsible for learner readiness.

EDUCATION ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

Two education contexts. One human-skill readiness agenda.

01 · SCHOOLS

School Engagement

Explore human-skill readiness across the school journey, with attention to communication, confidence, thinking, adaptability and the capabilities learners need as they progress toward higher education and future work.

02 · HIGHER EDUCATION

Higher Education Engagement

Apply ISSR 2030 insights to graduate readiness, employability, communication capability, placement preparation, institutional capability and education-to-workforce alignment.

APPLICATION AREAS

Where Education Engagement can begin.

Student Readiness

Understand the human capabilities learners may need to strengthen for academic progression, employability and future environments.

Critical Thinking & Adaptability

04

ENGAGEMENT PATHWAY

From research insight to measurable readiness.
