Publication Perspective

The future of work may be increasingly technological.

The capability to thrive within it remains deeply human.

ISSR-2030 presents a practitioner-driven synthesis of global research, field evidence and observed capability requirements.

It translates recurring capability themes into a structured human-capability architecture — a research-led perspective on the capabilities that will shape people, work and organisations in an AI-accelerated world.

Independent Research Framework

Published by Roushan Research International, ISSR-2030 draws upon the broader global evidence landscape without claiming endorsement or validation by external organisations.

Research Foundation & Methodology

From observed practice to a coherent capability architecture.

ISSR-2030 is grounded in more than 20 years of practitioner engagement with soft-skill research and development, particularly communication and professional capability.

Its practitioner research base includes approximately 320,000 survey respondents alongside long-term practitioner observation, learner interaction, training experience, behavioural insight, secondary research, crosswalk and synthesis.

  1. 01 Field Evidence
  2. 02 Practitioner Insight
  3. 03 Global Research Crosswalk
  4. 04 Emerging Capability Patterns
  5. 05 ISSR-2030 Architecture

The methodology is presented as a practitioner-led research synthesis. It is not positioned as a conventional statistically representative global survey.

Major Findings

Seven signals for an AI-accelerated world.

Together, the findings frame future readiness not as a collection of isolated traits, but as an observable, developable human capability system.

  1. 01

    Technical qualification does not automatically translate into professional effectiveness.

  2. 02

    Communication functions as a foundational capability.

  3. 03

    Human capability is interconnected.

  4. 04

    Capability must be observable and developable.

  5. 05

    AI increases the importance of human judgment.

  6. 06

    Practice and measurement matter.

  7. 07

    Future readiness is a human capability system.
